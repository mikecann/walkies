Concept art for "Walkies", an iPhone app that turns dog walks into fog-of-war exploration of your real suburb. The goal is to uncover 100% of it.

The view is a realistic satellite photo of Geographe in Busselton, Western Australia, seen from directly above like Apple Maps satellite view: flat imagery, no 3D modelled buildings. White sand beach and turquoise Geographe Bay along the top, Port Geographe marina and canal estate with jetties and boats on the right, the Vasse Estuary and wetlands at the bottom, streets of single-storey houses with terracotta and grey roofs in between. The attached map screenshots show the real layout of the streets, canals and coast; use them for geography only, not for style.

Every part of the suburb's land that has not been walked is hidden under thick, soft, fluffy, stylised white game clouds that cast gentle shadows on the ground. The open bay and estuary outside the suburb are clear. A walked corridor about three or four streets wide winds from the marina across the suburb and is completely clear and sunlit; it makes up about a third of the land. A tiny walker with a small dog on a lead stands at the end of the corridor, at the cloud edge. A frosted-glass iOS card at the top reads "Geographe" and "33%" with a thin progress bar, and an orange capsule button at the bottom reads "Start walk". Portrait phone screen, full bleed, no device frame, no watermark. Spell all text exactly as given.

Make it read unmistakably as strategy-game fog of war: the clouds are continuous with no gaps, and the cloud edge follows a hidden hexagon grid, so the outline of the walked corridor is clearly stepped like a honeycomb while staying soft and puffy.
