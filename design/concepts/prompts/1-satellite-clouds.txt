Concept art for "Walkies", an iPhone app that turns dog walks into a strategy-game style exploration of your real suburb. The map is covered in fog of war, and it clears wherever you have walked. The goal is to uncover 100% of the suburb.

The place is Geographe in Busselton, Western Australia: a long strip of single-storey houses with terracotta and grey tin roofs, white sand beach and turquoise water of Geographe Bay along the top edge, a marina and canal estate with jetties and small boats on the right, the Vasse Estuary and paperbark wetlands to the south, peppermint trees and lawns. The attached map is only a guide to the real layout: where the coast, canals, marina and streets are, and the hex-tile fog. Do not copy its flat styling, colours or UI.

Make it a single portrait phone game screen, full bleed, no device frame, no watermark. The person has explored about a third of the suburb so far: a winding band of walked streets is uncovered, the rest is hidden. Show a tiny walker with a small dog on a lead at the edge of the uncovered area. Any text must be spelled exactly as given.

Style: something buildable on top of a real map. Use a realistic, clean 3D Apple Maps style satellite-flyover view of the suburb, tilted about 45 degrees, with real-looking roofs, pools, trees and boats. Unexplored areas are hidden under soft, stylised, fluffy white game clouds that cast gentle shadows on the ground. Walked areas are clear and sunlit, with a faint glowing hex outline along the edge of the cloud line. Minimal frosted-glass iOS interface: a card at the top reading "Geographe" and "33%" with a progress bar, and an orange capsule button at the bottom reading "Start walk".
