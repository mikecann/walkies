Make walking interesting again

I built Walkies to make walking Sammy, my chocolate lab, a bit more interesting. My mate James, the CTO at Convex, told me about an app he was making where you try to walk every street in your city. I never got to see it, so I built my own.

Walkies puts your whole suburb under clouds and clears them off the streets you actually walk. The streets you haven't been down yet are drawn across the clouds as dotted lines, so there's always a reason to turn left instead of right.

Mike Cann, who made Walkies

Three reasons to take the long way round

Learn the stories behind your streets

Around Busselton in WA there are 136 secrets hidden under the tiles, spread across about 30 suburbs. Most are little bits of local history, with some Wardandi Noongar heritage and nature mixed in. Nothing on the map gives them away, so you find them by walking. Walk over one and it pops up, or you get a notification if your phone's in your pocket, and it goes in your secrets book with its own sticker.

Live somewhere else? You still get the clouds and the tiles, just no secrets yet.

{{phone secrets}}

See every walk play back

When you finish a walk it plays back over the map, with your route drawing itself and the clouds popping off each new tile. Then it guesses how many more walks like it you'll need to finish the suburb. Every walk is kept with its route, distance, time and steps.

It all saves on your phone first, so it works without signal, then backs up to your account when it can.

{{phone walks}}

Get something back for going further

You earn a shell for every new tile, and more for finding secrets, walking a few days in a row, reaching milestones like 50% of a suburb and finishing the three quests you get each week. Shells add up to levels, and every level lets you pick a reward.

Spend them in the shop on a new map marker, cloud colours, a trail for your walks, or a spyglass, which puts a ring on the map for three hours somewhere around a secret you haven't found yet.

How it works

  1. {{icon map-trifold}}

    Open it near home

    Your suburb adds itself from where you are and disappears under clouds on Apple's satellite map. Only tiles with a street or path count, so you never need to swim a lake.

  2. {{icon sneaker-move}}

    Go for a walk

    With Always on, Walkies notices when you head out on foot and records the walk by itself, phone in your pocket. Or switch that off and press Start walk.

  3. {{icon cloud}}

    Watch the clouds clear

    Every tile you walk through clears, your suburb's percentage goes up and you earn shells. When it's done, add the suburb next door and keep going.

Questions

Is Walkies free?{{icon caret-down}}

Yes. It's free on iPhone and there are no ads. The shells you spend in the shop are earned by walking.

What do I need to use it?{{icon caret-down}}

An iPhone on iOS 18 or later. You sign in with Apple or Google so your walks back up to your account.

Does it work where I live?{{icon caret-down}}

The clouds and tiles work in suburbs OpenStreetMap has a boundary for, and it's been tuned for Australia and England. The 136 secrets are only around Busselton in WA for now.

Do bike rides or drives count?{{icon caret-down}}

No, so there's no clearing your suburb from the car window. Walking and jogging count as long as you're under about 12 km/h, and if the phone's motion chip reckons you're cycling or driving it doesn't count however slow you go.

Why is there still cloud over the bush or the water?{{icon caret-down}}

Only tiles with a street or path count towards your 100%, so bush, wetland and water never stop you finishing. Those patches clear all at once when you walk a tile next to them.

Will it flatten my battery?{{icon caret-down}}

Always on keeps an eye on your location in the background, so it uses some battery, but GPS pauses while you're sitting still. If you'd rather it didn't run between walks, switch Always on off in Settings and press Start walk instead.

Does it work without signal?{{icon caret-down}}

Yes. Everything saves on your phone first and backs up to your account whenever there's signal.

What happens when I get a new phone?{{icon caret-down}}

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account and everything comes back.

What if I don't walk a street again for a while?{{icon caret-down}}

After two months a tile goes a bit misty, so you can see which bits you haven't been back to. It doesn't take anything off your percentage or your shells, and walking through it again clears it.

Can I walk with friends?{{icon caret-down}}

You can start a team of up to eight people and send them an invite link. For now a team is just who's in it, with everyone's name and colour. Your clouds and secrets stay your own.

How do I delete my account?{{icon caret-down}}

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. It deletes your account and everything in it from the server and clears the phone. The privacy policy has the details.
