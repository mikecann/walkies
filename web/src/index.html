Walk your suburb and the clouds lift off it.

Walkies is an iPhone app that hides your suburb under clouds on a satellite map. Go for a walk and the clouds clear off every street you walk down. The aim is to get your whole suburb to 100%, which is a lot harder than it sounds.

Free, iPhone only for now, and no ads.

How it works

  1. Add your suburb

    Type in your suburb or town and it turns up on the map under a blanket of cloud, split into hex tiles about 90 metres across. Your streets are dotted over the top so you can plan where to go.

  2. Go for a walk

    Pop your phone in your pocket and walk. Every tile you walk through clears. Bikes and cars don't count, so no cheating from the car window (I did think about it).

  3. Find the secrets

    Little bits of local history and nature are hidden under the clouds. Walk over one and it goes in your sticker book. Right now they're all around Busselton in WA, with more places to come.

A few other bits

  • Always on. If you let it, Walkies notices when you head out on foot and records the walk by itself, so you don't have to remember to press anything.
  • Steps. Each walk keeps its step count, from the phone's motion chip, so there's no extra drain on the battery.
  • Shells, levels and quests. New tiles earn shells, which you can spend on hats for your marker, cloud colours and a spyglass to peek at a secret.
  • Teams. Start a team with your mates and send them an invite link. Right now it's mostly names, colours and faces, with more to come. Your clouds and secrets stay yours.
  • Works offline. Everything's saved on your phone first, and backs up to your account when there's signal, so a new phone gets it all back.
