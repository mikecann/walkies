Things people ask

Why is my walk not clearing anything?

Walkies needs your location while you walk. Check it's allowed in the iPhone's Settings app, under Privacy & Security > Location Services > Walkies. It ignores anything faster than a jog (about 12 km/h), anything the phone thinks is a bike or a car, and GPS fixes that are too fuzzy, which can happen between tall buildings or under thick trees.

Do I have to press Start walk every time?

No. Turn on Always on in Settings and set Walkies' location permission to "Always", and it'll notice when you head out on foot and record the walk by itself. A walk ends after ten minutes without moving. If you'd rather it didn't, turn Always on off and press Start walk when you go.

Why are there clouds over the bush, the beach or the water?

Only tiles with a street or path through them count towards your 100%, so you're never stuck needing to swim across a lake. The bits with no path clear when you walk the street nearest to them. If you walk through a tile that wasn't counted, like a beach, it gets added and counts too.

Where are the secrets?

Each one is hidden under a tile near where its story happened, with nothing on the map to give it away. Right now they're all around Busselton in Western Australia. Other places get the clouds and the tiles, and I'm hoping to add secrets for more places over time.

Will it flatten my battery?

Less than you'd think. GPS only runs while you're walking, and with Always on it pauses while you're sitting still. Steps come from the phone's motion chip, which doesn't cost anything extra.

I've got a new phone. Will I lose everything?

No. Everything backs up to your Walkies account when there's signal. Sign in on the new phone with the same Apple or Google account and it all comes back.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. It asks twice, signs you out, clears the phone and deletes everything from Walkies' server. The privacy policy has the details. If you can't get into the app, email me from the address you signed in with and I'll do it for you.

Not yet. It's iPhone only for now.
